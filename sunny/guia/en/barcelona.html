Day 1

Sagrada Família⏱ about 3hGaudí's basilica, still under construction.
Park Güell⏱ about 1h30Gaudí's colourful park with city views.
Gothic Quarter⏱ about 2hMedieval lanes and the cathedral.

Day 2

La Rambla and La Boqueria market⏱ about 2hThe famous boulevard and the colourful food market.
Casa Batlló and Casa Milà⏱ about 1h30Gaudí's buildings on Passeig de Gràcia.
Barceloneta Beach⏱ about 3hCity beach with seafood restaurants.

Day 3

Montjuïc and the Magic Fountain⏱ about 45 minCable car, museums and the evening water show.

Day trips

  • Montserrat: Mountain monastery reached by rack railway. (Train and rack railway, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Girona: Medieval walled city with a Jewish quarter. (High-speed train, about 40 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Tapas and pa amb tomàquet
  • Seafood paella
  • Crema catalana
  • Afternoon vermouth

Tips

  • Book Sagrada Família and Park Güell in advance.
  • On La Rambla and the metro, watch for pickpockets.
  • The Hola Barcelona card gives unlimited transport.

From the airport to the centre

Aerobus or train to the centre, about 35 minutes.
