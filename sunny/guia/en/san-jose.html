Day 2

Jade Museum⏱ about 2h30The largest collection of American jade.

Day trips

  • Arenal Volcano and La Fortuna: Hot springs, hanging bridges and a waterfall. (Bus or shuttle, about 3 hours.)
  • Monteverde: Cloud forest and ziplines. (Shuttle, about 3.5 hours.)
  • Manuel Antonio: Beaches with sloths and monkeys. (Bus, about 3.5 hours.)

What to eat

  • Gallo pinto
  • Casado
  • Costa Rican coffee

Tips

  • San José is a base; the best is in nature.
  • December to April is dry season.

From the airport to the centre

Official taxi or Uber downtown, about 30 minutes.
