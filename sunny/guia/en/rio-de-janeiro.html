Day 1

Christ the Redeemer⏱ about 1h30Corcovado train up to the statue above the city.
Sugarloaf Mountain⏱ about 1h30Cable car with bay views, lovely at sunset.
Copacabana and Ipanema beaches⏱ about 3hPromenade, iced mate and the Arpoador rock.

Day 2

Selarón Steps and Lapa⏱ about 1h30Tiles from around the world and samba at night.
Botanical Garden⏱ about 2h30Imperial palms with Christ in the background.
Museum of Tomorrow and Olympic Boulevard⏱ about 2h30Futuristic museum and giant murals at the port.

Day 3

Santa Teresa⏱ about 1h30Artists' hilltop district and yellow tram.
Maracanã⏱ about 1h30Stadium tour or a football match.

Day trips

  • Petrópolis: The imperial city in the mountains. (Bus, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Arraial do Cabo: Turquoise-water beaches. (Bus or tour, about 3 hours.)
  • Ilha Grande: Car-free island, trails and Lopes Mendes. (Bus and boat, about 3 hours.)

What to eat

  • Feijoada (on Saturdays)
  • Biscoito Globo with mate
  • Açaí
  • Caipirinha

Tips

  • At the beach, bring only essentials, nothing valuable.
  • Book a timed ticket for Christ the Redeemer.
  • Use a ride app at night.

From the airport to the centre

Express bus, BRT or ride app to the South Zone, 40 to 60 minutes; from Santos Dumont (SDU), 20 minutes.
