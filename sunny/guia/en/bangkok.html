Chao Phraya river boat⏱ about 3hThe nicest way to get around.

Chinatown (Yaowarat)⏱ about 1h30Street food at night.

Day trips

  • Ayutthaya: Ruins of the old Siamese capital. (Train or van, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Damnoen Saduak Floating Market: Boats selling fruit and food. (Tour, about 1.5 hours.)

What to eat

  • Pad thai
  • Tom yum
  • Mango sticky rice
  • Green curry

Tips

  • Cover shoulders and knees at temples.
  • Use Grab or Bolt rather than unpriced tuk-tuks.
  • Ignore people saying the palace is closed.

From the airport to the centre

Airport Rail Link to Phaya Thai, about 30 minutes.
