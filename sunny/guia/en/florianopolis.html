Day 1

Northern beaches (Jurerê and Canasvieiras)⏱ about 3hCalm sea and full facilities.
Lagoa da Conceição⏱ about 3hRestaurants, dunes and lagoon sports.

Day 2

Joaquina Beach and dunes⏱ about 3hSurfing and dune sandboarding.
Ribeirão da Ilha⏱ about 3hAzorean village famous for oysters.

Day 3

Hercílio Luz Bridge and centre⏱ about 45 minThe lit bridge and the Public Market.
Lagoinha do Leste⏱ about 3hWild beach reached by trail.

Day trips

  • Guarda do Embaú: River meeting the sea, with surf. (Car, about 1 hour.)
  • Balneário Camboriú: City beach, cable car and big wheel. (Car, about 1¼ hours.)

What to eat

  • Baked oysters
  • Shrimp feast (sequência de camarão)
  • Grilled mullet

Tips

  • Rent a car: beaches are spread around the island.
  • In summer, bridges and roads jam.
  • Southern beaches have colder water.

From the airport to the centre

Ride app or bus to the centre, about 20 minutes; north of the island, 40 minutes.
