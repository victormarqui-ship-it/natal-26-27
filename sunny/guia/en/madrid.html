Day 1

Prado Museum⏱ about 3hVelázquez, Goya and El Greco in one of the world's great museums.
Royal Palace⏱ about 2h30The Spanish royal palace, with lavish halls.

Day 2

Retiro Park⏱ about 1h30Boating lake and the Crystal Palace.
Plaza Mayor and Puerta del Sol⏱ about 45 minThe lively historic heart of the city.
San Miguel Market⏱ about 2hTapas and wine in an early 20th-century iron market.

Day 3

Reina Sofía Museum⏱ about 2h30Modern art, including Picasso's Guernica.

Day trips

  • Toledo: Medieval city of three cultures above the Tagus. (High-speed train, about 35 minutes.)
  • Segovia: Roman aqueduct and the castle that inspired Disney. (High-speed train, about 30 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Calamari sandwich
  • Churros with chocolate
  • Cocido madrileño
  • Iberian ham

Tips

  • Locals dine late, after 9 pm.
  • The Prado is free in its last opening hours.
  • In summer, sightsee early: the heat is intense.

From the airport to the centre

Metro line 8 or the Exprés Aeropuerto bus, about 40 minutes.
