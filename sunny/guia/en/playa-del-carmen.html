What to eat

  • Fish tacos

Tips

  • Colectivos are the cheap local transport.
  • Carry pesos for the cenotes.
  • Avoid the strong sun from 11 am to 3 pm.

ADO bus or shuttle from Cancún airport, about 1 hour.
