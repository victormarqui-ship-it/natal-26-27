Day 1

Hallgrímskirkja⏱ about 1h30The iconic church with city views.
Harpa⏱ about 1h30Glass concert hall by the sea.
Blue Lagoon⏱ about 3hMilky-blue geothermal waters in a lava field.

Day 2

Northern lights⏱ about 1h30September to March, away from city lights.
Whale watching⏱ about 3hBoats leave from the old harbour.

Day trips

  • Golden Circle: Geysir, Gullfoss falls and Þingvellir. (Car or tour, full day.)
  • South Coast and Reynisfjara beach: Waterfalls and a black-sand beach. (Car or tour, full day.)

What to eat

  • Lamb soup
  • Pylsur (hot dog)
  • Skyr

Tips

  • At Reynisfjara keep away from the water: sneaker waves.
  • Book the Blue Lagoon in advance.
  • Tap water is excellent: skip bottled water.

From the airport to the centre

Flybus to the centre, about 45 minutes.
