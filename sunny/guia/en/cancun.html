Underwater Museum (MUSA)⏱ about 2h30Underwater statues by snorkel.

Day trips

  • Chichén Itzá: The Mayan pyramid, a world wonder. (Tour, about 2.5 hours.)
  • Tulum and cenotes: Seaside ruins and natural sinkholes. (Car or bus, about 2 hours.)

What to eat

  • Tacos al pastor
  • Cochinita pibil
  • Ceviche
  • Guacamole

Tips

  • Use biodegradable sunscreen in cenotes and parks.
  • Sargassum seaweed can appear May to August.
  • Pay in pesos for better prices.

From the airport to the centre

Shuttle or ADO bus to the hotel zone, about 20 minutes.
