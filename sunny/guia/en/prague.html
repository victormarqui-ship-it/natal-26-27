Day 1

Prague Castle⏱ about 2h30The world's largest castle complex, with St. Vitus Cathedral.
Charles Bridge⏱ about 45 minStone bridge with statues, lovely at dawn.
Old Town Square and Astronomical Clock⏱ about 2hThe medieval clock that comes alive every hour.

Day 2

Jewish Quarter (Josefov)⏱ about 2hSynagogues and the old Jewish cemetery.
Malá Strana and Petřín Hill⏱ about 1h30Baroque district and viewpoint with funicular.

Day trips

  • Český Krumlov: Medieval town with a riverside castle. (Bus, about 3 hours.)
  • Kutná Hora: The bone chapel and St. Barbara's Cathedral. (Train, about 1 hour.)

What to eat

  • Svíčková
  • Goulash with dumplings
  • Trdelník
  • Pilsner beer

Tips

  • Change money only at trusted offices, never on the street.
  • Use the trams: cheap and frequent.
  • Visit Charles Bridge early to avoid crowds.

From the airport to the centre

Bus 119 plus metro, or the AE bus, about 40 minutes.
