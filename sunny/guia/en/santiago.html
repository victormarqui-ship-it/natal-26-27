Day trips

  • Valparaíso and Viña del Mar: Colourful hills, funiculars and beaches. (Bus, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Valle Nevado and Farellones: Snow from June to September. (Shuttle, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Maipo Valley wineries: Concha y Toro and other wineries. (Metro or tour, about 1 hour.)

What to eat

  • Empanada de pino
  • Pastel de choclo
  • Pisco sour
  • Carménère wine

Tips

  • Andes snow season is June to September.
  • Use the bip! card on the metro.
  • Many museums close on Mondays.

From the airport to the centre

Centropuerto or Turbus bus to the centre, about 30 minutes.
