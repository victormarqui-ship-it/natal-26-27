Day 1

Paulista Avenue and MASP⏱ about 45 minThe suspended museum and the avenue open to pedestrians on Sundays.
Ibirapuera Park⏱ about 1h30Museums, lakes and the city's green lung.
Municipal Market⏱ about 2hThe mortadella sandwich and the stained glass.
Batman Alley and Vila Madalena⏱ about 1h30Graffiti, bars and boutiques.

Day 2

Pinacoteca⏱ about 2h30The city's oldest art museum.
Liberdade⏱ about 1h30The Japanese district, with a weekend fair.
Football Museum⏱ about 2h30Inside the Pacaembu stadium.

Day trips

  • Holambra: The flower town with a Dutch windmill. (Car, about 2 hours.)
  • Santos: Beach, Coffee Museum and Pelé Museum. (Bus, about 1¼ hours.)

What to eat

  • São Paulo pizza
  • Mortadella sandwich
  • Market pastel
  • Japanese food in Liberdade

Tips

  • Traffic is heavy: use the metro whenever possible.
  • On Sundays Paulista Avenue closes to cars.
  • Book famous restaurants ahead.

From the airport to the centre

Airport Bus Service, train (line 13) or ride app, 45 to 90 minutes; from Congonhas (CGH), 20 to 40 minutes.
