Day 1

Belém Tower and Jerónimos Monastery⏱ about 2h30The heart of Lisbon's Age of Discovery.
Pastéis de Belém⏱ about 1h30The original home of the custard tart, next to the monastery.
Alfama and São Jorge Castle⏱ about 2h30Medieval alleys, fado and the city's best view. Lots of hills.

Day 2

Lisbon Oceanarium⏱ about 2h30One of Europe's largest aquariums, in Parque das Nações.
Baixa, Rua Augusta and Praça do Comércio⏱ about 45 minThe flat, elegant centre on the Tagus river.
Viewpoints and Bairro Alto at sunset⏱ about 2hRiver viewpoints and lively streets at night.

Day 3

Time Out Market⏱ about 2hFood hall with stalls from the city's best chefs.
National Tile Museum⏱ about 3hFive centuries of Portuguese tiles.
LX Factory⏱ about 1h30Old factory with shops, a bookstore and restaurants.

What to eat

  • Custard tart (pastel de nata)
  • Bacalhau à Brás (salt cod)
  • Bifana (pork sandwich)
  • Grilled sardines (in summer)
  • Ginjinha (cherry liqueur)

Tips

  • Portuguese cobblestones are slippery in the rain: wear rubber soles.
  • Tram 28 gets packed and attracts pickpockets; go early.
  • The rechargeable transport card (Navegante ocasional) works on metro, buses and trams.

From the airport to the centre

Metro (red line) or taxi to the centre, about 20–30 minutes.
