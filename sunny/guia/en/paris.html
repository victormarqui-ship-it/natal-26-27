Paris in 3 days

Day 1

Eiffel Tower⏱ about 45 minThe symbol of Paris; go up late afternoon to see the lights.
Louvre Museum⏱ about 3hMona Lisa, Venus de Milo and the world's largest collection.
Notre-Dame Cathedral and Île de la Cité⏱ about 1hThe restored cathedral and the islands in the Seine.

Day 2

Montmartre and Sacré-Cœur⏱ about 1h30The painters' quarter, with city views from the hilltop.
Musée d'Orsay⏱ about 2h30The Impressionists in a former railway station.

Day 3

Seine river cruise⏱ about 3hThe main sights seen from the river in an hour.
Le Marais⏱ about 1h30Medieval streets, shops, cafés and Place des Vosges.
Luxembourg Gardens⏱ about 1h30Classic garden with toy boats for children.

Day trips

  • Palace of Versailles: Louis XIV's palace and its vast gardens. (RER C train, about 40 minutes.)
  • Disneyland Paris: Two theme parks, ideal with children. (RER A train, about 40 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Croissant and pain au chocolat
  • Crêpe
  • French cheese and wine
  • Macarons

Tips

  • Book timed tickets for the Louvre and the Eiffel Tower.
  • Watch out for pickpockets on the metro.
  • Many museums close on Mondays or Tuesdays: check first.

From the airport to the centre

RER B train or Roissybus to the centre, about 45 minutes.
