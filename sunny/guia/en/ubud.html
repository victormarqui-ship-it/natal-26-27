Day trips

  • Uluwatu and Tanah Lot temples: Sea temples at sunset. (Car with driver, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Nusa Penida: Kelingking Beach and manta snorkelling. (Fast boat, about 45 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Nasi goreng
  • Babi guling
  • Satay

Tips

  • Hiring a driver for the day is cheap and easy.
  • Wear a sarong at temples.
  • Don't show food to the monkeys.

From the airport to the centre

Taxi or transfer from Bali airport, about 1.5 hours.
