Day trips

  • Monaco: Monte Carlo casino, harbour and the prince's palace. (Train, about 25 minutes.)
  • Èze: Medieval village perched on a cliff above the sea. (Bus, about 30 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Socca
  • Salade niçoise
  • Pissaladière
  • Artisan ice cream

Tips

  • Bring water shoes: the beaches are pebbly.
  • The coastal train links Nice, Monaco and Cannes quickly.
  • In July and August, book hotels early.

From the airport to the centre

Tram line 2 to the centre, about 25 minutes.
