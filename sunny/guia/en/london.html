Day 1

Big Ben and Westminster Abbey⏱ about 1hParliament and the church of royal coronations.
British Museum⏱ about 2h30Rosetta Stone and Egyptian mummies, free entry.
Tower of London and Tower Bridge⏱ about 45 minThe Crown Jewels and the city's most famous bridge.

Day 2

Buckingham Palace⏱ about 2h30The Changing of the Guard on selected mornings.
London Eye and South Bank⏱ about 1h30Big wheel and riverside walk along the Thames.
Natural History Museum⏱ about 2h30Dinosaurs and a stunning building, free.

Day 3

Camden Market and Covent Garden⏱ about 2hMarkets, street food and street performers.

Day trips

  • Windsor: The castle where the royal family spends weekends. (Train, about 1 hour.)
  • Stonehenge and Bath: The prehistoric stone circle and the Roman baths. (Coach tour, full day.)

What to eat

  • Fish and chips
  • Full English breakfast
  • Afternoon tea with scones
  • Indian food on Brick Lane

Tips

  • Tap a contactless card on the Tube: there is a daily cap.
  • The major museums are free.
  • Carry an umbrella in any season.

From the airport to the centre

Tube (Piccadilly or Elizabeth line) or Heathrow Express, 15 to 50 minutes.
