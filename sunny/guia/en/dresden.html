Day trips

  • Saxon Switzerland and Bastei: Rock formations and the Bastei Bridge. (S-Bahn S1, about 40 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Eierschecke
  • Dresden Stollen

Tips

  • In December, the Striezelmarkt is one of the oldest Christmas markets.
  • The old town is compact and walkable.
  • The Zwinger and Military Museum close some weekdays.

From the airport to the centre

S-Bahn S2 to the centre, about 20 minutes.
