Day 1

Main Market Square (Rynek)⏱ about 2hOne of Europe's largest medieval squares.
Wawel Castle⏱ about 2h30The royal castle and cathedral above the river.
Kazimierz⏱ about 1h30The old Jewish quarter, now full of cafés.

Day 2

Schindler's Factory⏱ about 1h30Museum on the Nazi occupation.

Day trips

  • Auschwitz-Birkenau: Memorial of the largest Nazi death camp. (Bus or tour, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Wieliczka Salt Mine: Chapels carved from salt, underground. (Train, about 30 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Pierogi
  • Zapiekanka
  • Obwarzanek
  • Żurek

Tips

  • Book a guided Auschwitz visit well ahead.
  • The currency is the złoty; prices are low.
  • The whole centre is walkable.

From the airport to the centre

Train to the main station, about 20 minutes.
