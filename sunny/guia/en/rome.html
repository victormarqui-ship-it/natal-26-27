Day 1

Colosseum and Roman Forum⏱ about 3hThe heart of ancient Rome. Timed entry.
Vatican Museums and Sistine Chapel⏱ about 3hOne of the world's largest art collections.

Day 2

St Peter's Basilica⏱ about 1hFree entry; shoulders and knees covered.
Trevi Fountain and Pantheon⏱ about 45 minBest early morning or at night, with fewer people.
Trastevere at night⏱ about 1h30The neighbourhood of trattorias and cobbled streets.
Piazza Navona and Campo de' Fiori⏱ about 1h30Baroque squares and the morning market.

Day 3

Villa Borghese⏱ about 1h30Huge park with bikes and the Borghese Gallery.

What to eat

  • Carbonara
  • Cacio e pepe
  • Supplì (rice croquettes)
  • Pizza al taglio (by the slice)
  • Gelato

Tips

  • Street fountains (nasoni) have free drinking water: bring a bottle.
  • Churches and the Vatican require covered shoulders and knees.
  • Watch out for pickpockets on the metro and near monuments.

From the airport to the centre

Leonardo Express train to Termini station, 32 minutes.
