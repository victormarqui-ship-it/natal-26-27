Day 2

Lake Lucerne boat trip⏱ about 3hPaddle steamers among mountains.

Day trips

  • Monte Pilatus: The world's steepest cogwheel railway and cable car. (Boat and train, about 1 hour.)
  • Monte Rigi: Queen of the mountains, overlooking 13 lakes. (Boat and train, about 1¼ hours.)

What to eat

  • Luzerner Chügelipastete
  • Fondue
  • Swiss chocolate

Tips

  • With a Swiss Travel Pass the lake boats are free.
  • Check the forecast before going up the mountains.
  • The old town is small and walkable.

From the airport to the centre

Train from Zurich Airport, about 1 hour.
