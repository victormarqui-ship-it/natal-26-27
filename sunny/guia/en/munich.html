Day 1

Marienplatz⏱ about 3hThe main square and the Town Hall glockenspiel.
English Garden⏱ about 1h30Huge park with river surfers and beer gardens.

Day 2

Nymphenburg Palace⏱ about 2h30Summer residence of the Bavarian kings.
Viktualienmarkt⏱ about 1h30Open-air market with food and beer.
Hofbräuhaus⏱ about 1h30The world's most famous beer hall.

Day 3

Deutsches Museum⏱ about 2h30One of the largest science and technology museums.

Day trips

  • Neuschwanstein Castle: The fairy-tale castle in the Alps. (Train and bus, about 2.5 hours.)
  • Salzburg: Mozart's city, in Austria. (Train, about 1.5 hours.)

What to eat

  • Weisswurst with pretzel
  • Schweinshaxe (pork knuckle)
  • Apfelstrudel
  • Wheat beer

Tips

  • The Bayern-Ticket covers regional trains all day.
  • Book Neuschwanstein in advance.
  • Oktoberfest starts in September: hotel prices triple.

From the airport to the centre

S-Bahn S1 or S8 to the centre, about 40 minutes.
