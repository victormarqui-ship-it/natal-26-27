Day trips

  • Aconcágua: The Americas' highest mountain, with a lookout. (High-mountain tour, full day.)

What to eat

  • Malbec wine
  • Asado
  • Mendoza empanadas

Tips

  • Book wineries in advance.
  • Bikes are great for Maipú wineries.
  • Harvest festival (Vendimia) is in March.

From the airport to the centre

Taxi or bus to the centre, about 20 minutes.
