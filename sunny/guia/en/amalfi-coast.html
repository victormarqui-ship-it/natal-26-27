Day 1

Positano⏱ about 1h30The village cascading down to the sea. Many stairs.
Amalfi and its cathedral⏱ about 1hThe town that names the coast, with St Andrew's cathedral.
Ravello and Villa Rufolo⏱ about 1h30Mountaintop gardens with sea views.

Day 2

Ferry between the villages⏱ about 3hThe calmest, prettiest way to move along the coast.
Beaches and boat trip⏱ about 3hClear-water coves; boats leave from Positano and Amalfi.

Day 3

Path of the Gods⏱ about 3hAbout a 3-hour hillside hike with amazing views.

What to eat

  • Limoncello
  • Delizia al limone (lemon cake)
  • Seafood
  • Neapolitan pizza in Naples

Tips

  • The road is narrow and buses fill up in summer: take the ferry.
  • The season runs April to October; in winter many hotels and ferries stop.

From the airport to the centre

Bus or transfer to Sorrento or Salerno, then ferry or SITA bus to the villages.
