Day 1

Train between the five villages⏱ about 1h30Monterosso, Vernazza, Corniglia, Manarola and Riomaggiore, a few minutes apart.
Sentiero Azzurro trail⏱ about 3hThe classic hillside trail. Some sections close; check beforehand.
Vernazza and Manarola⏱ about 1h30The most photographed villages, best in late afternoon.

Day 2

Monterosso beach⏱ about 3hThe area's only sandy beach.
Boat between the villages⏱ about 3hRuns in season, with views of the villages from the sea.

What to eat

  • Genoese pesto
  • Focaccia
  • Monterosso anchovies
  • Sciacchetrà wine

Tips

  • The Cinque Terre Card includes trains between villages and the trails.
  • The villages have many stairs and slopes: hard with a stroller or limited mobility.
  • Staying in La Spezia is usually cheaper.

From the airport to the centre

Train to La Spezia, then regional train to the villages.
