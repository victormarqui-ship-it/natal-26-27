Day trips

  • Canela: Caracol Falls and the stone cathedral. (Car, about 15 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Fondue
  • Homemade chocolate
  • Colonial-style tea buffet

Tips

  • Winter can drop below 5 °C.
  • Book fondue and café colonial on weekends.
  • Holidays and Natal Luz fill the town.
