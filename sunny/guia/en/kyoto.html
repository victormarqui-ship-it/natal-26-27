Day 1

Fushimi Inari Shrine⏱ about 3hThousands of red gates up the mountain.
Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)⏱ about 3hThe gold-covered temple on a pond.

Day 2

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove⏱ about 1h30A path through giant bamboo.
Gion and Higashiyama⏱ about 1h30Wooden lanes, geishas and Kiyomizu-dera temple.
Nishiki Market⏱ about 2hKyoto's kitchen in a covered arcade.

Day 3

Tea ceremony⏱ about 1h30Matcha made the traditional way.

Day trips

  • Nara: The Great Buddha temple and free-roaming deer. (Train, about 45 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Kaiseki
  • Tofu and yuba
  • Matcha
  • Wagashi sweets

Tips

  • Visit Fushimi Inari and Arashiyama very early.
  • Buses get packed; trains and bikes help.
  • Don't photograph geishas without permission.

From the airport to the centre

Haruka train from Kansai Airport, about 1h20.
