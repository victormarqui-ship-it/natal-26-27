Day 2

Anne Frank House⏱ about 1h30The hiding place where she wrote her diary.
Canal cruise⏱ about 3hThe 17th-century canals seen from the water.
Jordaan⏱ about 1h30Charming neighbourhood with cafés and boutiques.

Day 3

Vondelpark⏱ about 1h30The city's best-loved park.

Day trips

  • Zaanse Schans and Volendam: Windmills and fishing villages. (Bus or train, about 30 minutes.)
  • Keukenhof: The tulip garden, spring only. (Bus, about 45 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Stroopwafel
  • Raw herring
  • Bitterballen
  • Gouda cheese

Tips

  • Anne Frank House tickets are online only, weeks ahead.
  • Watch the bike lanes: bikes have priority.
  • For tulips, go mid-March to mid-May.

From the airport to the centre

Train to Central Station, about 17 minutes.
