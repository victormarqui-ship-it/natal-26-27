Day trips

  • Everglades: Airboat ride among alligators. (Car or tour, about 1 hour.)
  • Key West: The southern tip of the US via the Overseas Highway. (Car, about 3.5 hours.)

What to eat

  • Cuban sandwich
  • Stone crab
  • Key lime pie
  • Cuban coffee (cafecito)

Tips

  • A car helps, but Miami Beach parking is pricey.
  • June to November is hurricane season.
  • Many people speak Spanish.

From the airport to the centre

Metrorail or taxi downtown, about 20 minutes; Miami Beach 30 minutes.
