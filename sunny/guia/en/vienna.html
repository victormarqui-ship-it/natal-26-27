Day 1

Schönbrunn Palace⏱ about 3hHabsburg residence with gardens and a zoo.
St. Stephen's Cathedral⏱ about 1hThe colourful tiled roof in the centre.
Belvedere Palace⏱ about 2h30Klimt's The Kiss in a Baroque palace.

Day 2

Hofburg⏱ about 1h30The imperial palace and the Sisi Museum.
Vienna State Opera⏱ about 1h30Cheap standing tickets on the day.
Prater⏱ about 1h30Park with the historic giant wheel.

Day trips

  • Danube Valley (Wachau): Vineyards, Melk Abbey and a river boat. (Train, about 1¼ hours.)
  • Bratislava: Slovakia's capital, very close. (Train, about 1 hour.)

What to eat

  • Wiener Schnitzel
  • Sachertorte
  • Apfelstrudel
  • Viennese coffee

Tips

  • The Vienna City Card covers transport and discounts.
  • Traditional cafés are an outing in themselves.
  • Christmas markets open mid-November.

From the airport to the centre

CAT train or S-Bahn to the centre, 16 to 25 minutes.
