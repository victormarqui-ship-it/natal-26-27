Brandenburg Gate and Reichstag⏱ about 45 minSymbols of reunification; the glass dome is free with booking.

Day 2

Holocaust Memorial⏱ about 1h302,711 concrete slabs in memory of the victims.

Alexanderplatz TV Tower⏱ about 45 minThe highest view in the city.

Day trips

What to eat

  • Currywurst
  • Döner kebab
  • Berliner doughnut
  • German beer

Tips

  • The AB ticket covers most trips; ABC includes the airport.
  • Many places are cash only.
  • On Sundays most shops close.

From the airport to the centre

FEX train or S-Bahn to the centre, about 30 minutes.
