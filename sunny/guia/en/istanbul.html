What to eat

  • Kebab and döner
  • Baklava
  • Turkish coffee
  • Simit and tea

Tips

  • Women must cover their heads in mosques.
  • Use the Istanbulkart on metro, tram and ferries.
  • Haggling is expected in the bazaar.

From the airport to the centre

M11 metro or Havaist bus to the centre, 45 to 75 minutes.
