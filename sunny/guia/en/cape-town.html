Day 1

Table Mountain⏱ about 1h30Rotating cable car to the flat top.
V&A Waterfront⏱ about 1h30Harbour with shops, restaurants and aquarium.
Robben Island⏱ about 3hNelson Mandela's prison.

Day 2

Bo-Kaap⏱ about 1h30Colourful houses and Cape Malay food.
Kirstenbosch Gardens⏱ about 1h30Botanical garden with a canopy walkway.
Camps Bay and Clifton beaches⏱ about 3hWhite sand below the mountains.

Day trips

  • Cape of Good Hope and Boulders Beach: Beach penguins and the tip of the continent. (Car, about 1¼ hours.)
  • Stellenbosch and Franschhoek: The Cape Winelands. (Car or tour, about 1 hour.)

What to eat

  • Bobotie
  • Braai (barbecue)
  • Pinotage wine

Tips

  • Use Uber at night and avoid empty areas.
  • The cable car shuts in wind: check the forecast.
  • November to March is summer.

From the airport to the centre

MyCiTi bus or Uber downtown, about 25 minutes.
