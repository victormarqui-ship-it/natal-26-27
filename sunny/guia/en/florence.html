Day 1

Uffizi Gallery⏱ about 3hBotticelli, Leonardo and Michelangelo in one museum.
Michelangelo's David at the Accademia⏱ about 1h30The most famous statue of the Renaissance.
Duomo and Brunelleschi's dome⏱ about 1h30Climbing the dome is 463 steps, no lift.

Day 2

Ponte Vecchio and Oltrarno⏱ about 45 minThe jewellers' bridge and the artisans' quarter.
Piazzale Michelangelo at sunset⏱ about 1h30The classic view of the whole city.
Central Market⏱ about 2hUpper floor with all kinds of Tuscan food.
Boboli Gardens⏱ about 1h30Pitti Palace gardens, good for kids to run around.

What to eat

  • Bistecca alla fiorentina (T-bone)
  • Lampredotto (tripe sandwich)
  • Ribollita (bread soup)
  • Cantucci with vin santo

Tips

  • Book Uffizi and Accademia ahead to avoid hours in line.
  • The centre is a restricted traffic zone: don't rent a car for the city.

From the airport to the centre

T2 tram from Florence airport to the centre, about 20 minutes.
