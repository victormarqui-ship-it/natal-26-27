Day 1

St Mark's Square and Basilica⏱ about 1hThe heart of Venice, with golden mosaics.
Doge's Palace and Bridge of Sighs⏱ about 2h30The doges' palace and the old prisons.
Grand Canal by vaporetto⏱ about 1h30Line 1 runs the whole canal. Great for those who can't walk much.

Day 2

Rialto Bridge and fish market⏱ about 2hThe market runs in the morning, except Sunday.
Gondola ride⏱ about 1h30Pricey but unforgettable; the price is per gondola, not per person.
Peggy Guggenheim Collection⏱ about 1h30Modern art in a canal-side palace.

What to eat

  • Cicchetti with spritz
  • Squid-ink risotto
  • Sarde in saor (sweet-sour sardines)
  • Tiramisù

Tips

  • Venice charges a visitor access fee on peak days: check whether it applies to your dates.
  • Over 400 bridges with steps: small, light bags help a lot.
  • In autumn and winter there may be acqua alta (high tide); the hotel will warn you.

From the airport to the centre

Bus to Piazzale Roma or Alilaguna boat to the centre, about 30–60 minutes.
