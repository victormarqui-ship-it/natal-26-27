Day 1

Cusco's Plaza de Armas⏱ about 45 minCathedral and churches on Inca walls.
Qorikancha⏱ about 1h30The Inca Sun Temple beneath a convent.
Sacsayhuamán⏱ about 1h30Inca fortress of giant stones.
San Blas quarter⏱ about 2hArtisans' lanes and city views.

Day 2

San Pedro Market⏱ about 2hJuices, Andean fruits and local food.

Day trips

  • Machu Picchu: The lost city of the Incas. (Train to Aguas Calientes, about 3.5 hours, then bus.)
  • Sacred Valley: Pisac, Ollantaytambo and Moray. (Tour, full day.)
  • Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca): Striped mountain at 5,000 m. (Tour, full day.)

What to eat

  • Lomo saltado
  • Coca tea
  • Corn with cheese
  • Grilled alpaca

Tips

  • Cusco is at 3,400 m: rest on day one.
  • Book Machu Picchu and the train well ahead.
  • The Boleto Turístico covers many sites.

From the airport to the centre

Official taxi to the centre, about 20 minutes.
