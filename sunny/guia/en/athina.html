Day 1

Acropolis and Parthenon⏱ about 3hThe cradle of Western civilisation.
Acropolis Museum⏱ about 3hThe original sculptures, with glass floors over ruins.

Day 2

Plaka⏱ about 1h30The oldest district, with tavernas and shops.
Ancient Agora⏱ about 1h30Socrates' square and the Temple of Hephaestus.
Mount Lycabettus⏱ about 1h30The finest sunset over the city.
Changing of the guard at Syntagma⏱ about 45 minThe evzone guards in traditional dress.

Day trips

  • Cape Sounion: The Temple of Poseidon above the sea at sunset. (Bus or tour, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Delfos: The oracle's sanctuary in the mountains. (Tour, full day.)

What to eat

  • Souvlaki and gyros
  • Moussaka
  • Greek salad
  • Greek yogurt with honey

Tips

  • Climb the Acropolis early: strong sun and crowds.
  • The combined ticket covers several ancient sites.
  • Island ferries leave from Piraeus.

From the airport to the centre

Metro (blue line) to Syntagma, about 40 minutes.
