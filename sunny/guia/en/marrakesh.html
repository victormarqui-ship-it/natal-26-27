Day 2

Bahia Palace⏱ about 2h30Moroccan courtyards and tiles.
Koutoubia Mosque⏱ about 1hThe city's landmark minaret.
Traditional hammam⏱ about 1h30Steam bath and scrub.

Day trips

  • Atlas Mountains and Ourika: Berber villages and waterfalls. (Tour, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Agafay Desert: Stone-desert camp and camels. (Tour, about 45 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Tagine
  • Couscous
  • Mint tea
  • Pastilla

Tips

  • Haggling is part of souk shopping.
  • Decline 'help' finding your way in the medina.
  • Stay in a riad, a traditional house.

From the airport to the centre

Bus 19 or taxi to the Medina, about 20 minutes.
