Day 3

Day trips

  • Mount Fuji and Hakone: Lake, ropeway, hot springs and Fuji views. (Train, about 1.5 hours.)
  • Nikko: Golden temples in the mountains. (Train, about 2 hours.)
  • Kamakura: The Great Buddha and seaside temples. (Train, about 1 hour.)

What to eat

  • Sushi
  • Ramen
  • Tempura
  • Convenience-store treats

Tips

  • Use a Suica or Pasmo card on transport.
  • There is no tipping in Japan.
  • The Japan Rail Pass only pays off with many long trips.

From the airport to the centre

Monorail or Keikyu train downtown, about 30 minutes; from Narita (NRT), Narita Express in 1 hour.
