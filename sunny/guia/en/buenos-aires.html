Day 1

La Boca and Caminito⏱ about 1h30Colourful houses, street tango and the Boca stadium.
Recoleta and its cemetery⏱ about 1h30Striking mausoleums, including Evita's.
Plaza de Mayo and Casa Rosada⏱ about 45 minThe political heart and the Cathedral.
San Telmo and its Sunday fair⏱ about 2hAntiques, tango and historic bars.

Day 2

Tango show⏱ about 1h30Dinner with a professional tango show.

Day 3

El Ateneo Grand Splendid⏱ about 1h30A bookshop in an old theatre.

Day trips

  • Colonia del Sacramento (Uruguay): Colonial town across the river. (Ferry, about 1¼ hours (bring ID).)
  • Tigre and the Paraná Delta: Boat ride among islands and stilt houses. (Train, about 1 hour.)

What to eat

  • Bife de chorizo and parrilla
  • Empanadas
  • Dulce de leche and alfajores
  • Malbec wine

Tips

  • Pay with a foreign card or bring dollars: exchange rates swing.
  • Argentines dine late, after 9 pm.
  • Use the SUBE card on subway and buses.

From the airport to the centre

Bus or remis to the centre, 45 to 60 minutes; from Aeroparque (AEP), 15 minutes.
