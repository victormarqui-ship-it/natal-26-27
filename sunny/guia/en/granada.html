Day 1

Alhambra⏱ about 3hThe Nasrid palace and the Generalife gardens.
Albaicín⏱ about 1h30Moorish quarter with the San Nicolás viewpoint.
Sacromonte⏱ about 1h30Cave houses and flamenco in the caves.

Day 2

Cathedral and Royal Chapel⏱ about 1hResting place of the Catholic Monarchs.

Day trips

  • Sierra Nevada: Mountains with snow in winter and trails in summer. (Bus, about 1 hour.)

What to eat

  • Free tapas with your drink
  • Tortilla del Sacromonte
  • Moorish tea and sweets

Tips

  • Alhambra tickets sell out: buy weeks ahead.
  • Take the minibus up to the Albaicín.
  • In many bars each drink comes with a tapa.

From the airport to the centre

Bus to the centre, about 40 minutes.
