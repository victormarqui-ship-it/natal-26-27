Day 1

Royal Alcázar⏱ about 1h30Moorish palace with dreamlike gardens.
Cathedral and Giralda⏱ about 1hThe world's largest Gothic cathedral and its tower.
Plaza de España⏱ about 3hMonumental tiled square with rowing boats.

Day 2

Santa Cruz quarter⏱ about 2hWhite lanes, flowered patios and tapas bars.
Metropol Parasol⏱ about 1h30Wooden structure with a rooftop walkway.
Flamenco show⏱ about 1h30Authentic flamenco in Triana or the centre.

Day trips

  • Cordoba: The Mosque-Cathedral and the Jewish quarter. (High-speed train, about 45 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Salmorejo
  • Ham and cheese tapas
  • Fried fish
  • Torrijas and convent sweets

Tips

  • July and August top 40 °C: avoid midday.
  • Book a timed slot for the Alcázar.
  • Spring, with the April Fair, is lovely and busy.

From the airport to the centre

EA bus to the centre, about 35 minutes.
