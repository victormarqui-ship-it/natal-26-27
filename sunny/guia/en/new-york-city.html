What to eat

  • Pizza by the slice
  • Bagel with cream cheese
  • Pastrami at Katz's
  • Cheesecake

Tips

  • Pay the subway by tapping a card (OMNY).
  • Prices exclude tax and tipping is 18–20%.
  • Cheaper Broadway tickets at the TKTS booth.

From the airport to the centre

AirTrain plus subway to Manhattan, about 1 hour; taxi 45–75 minutes.
