Ribeira and Dom Luís I Bridge⏱ about 45 minPorto's postcard view on the Douro river.
Port wine cellars in Gaia⏱ about 1h30Tour with tasting in the historic cellars.

Day 2

Clérigos Tower⏱ about 45 min360-degree city view. 240 steps.
Crystal Palace Gardens⏱ about 2h30Garden with peacocks and river views.
Foz do Douro at sunset⏱ about 1h30Where the river meets the sea; ride the historic tram.

What to eat

  • Francesinha (Porto sandwich)
  • Cod fritters
  • Porto-style tripe
  • Port wine
  • Manteigaria custard tart

Tips

  • The city is steep: the Guindais funicular helps you up from Ribeira.
  • Bring a light jacket even in summer: it's windy by the river at night.

From the airport to the centre

Metro line E to the centre, about 30 minutes.
