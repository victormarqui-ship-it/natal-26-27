Day 1

Historic Centre of Lima⏱ about 2hPlaza Mayor, cathedral and San Francisco catacombs.
Miraflores and the Malecón⏱ about 1h30Cliff-top promenade over the Pacific.
Barranco⏱ about 1h30Bohemian district with street art and bars.

Day 2

Larco Museum⏱ about 2h30Pre-Columbian art in a colonial mansion.
Huaca Pucllana⏱ about 1h30Adobe pyramid in the middle of Miraflores.
Magic Water Circuit⏱ about 1h30Illuminated fountains at night.

Day trips

  • Paracas and the Ballestas Islands: Sea lions, penguins and desert. (Bus, about 3.5 hours.)

What to eat

  • Ceviche
  • Lomo saltado
  • Pisco sour
  • Anticuchos

Tips

  • Lima is a food capital: book a top restaurant.
  • Use ride apps.
  • June to October skies are grey (garúa).

From the airport to the centre

Airport Express bus or official taxi to Miraflores, about 45 minutes.
