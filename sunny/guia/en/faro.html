Day 2

Deserta Island⏱ about 3hWild beach at mainland Portugal's southernmost tip.

Day trips

  • Lagos and Ponta da Piedade: Golden cliffs and boat trips into caves. (Bus or train, about 1¾ hours.)
  • Benagil: The famous cave open to the sky. (Car, about 50 minutes.)

What to eat

  • Seafood cataplana
  • Grilled sardines
  • Almond and fig sweets

Tips

  • In the Algarve a rental car helps a lot.
  • The Atlantic is cold even in summer.
  • Island boats leave from Porta Nova pier.

From the airport to the centre

Bus or taxi to the centre, about 15 minutes.
